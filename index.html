Grooming

Keep your pet clean, healthy, and looking their best with our professional grooming services — from soothing medicated baths and brushing to precision nail trimming, ear cleaning, and styling.

Surgery

We offer safe and modern surgical procedures, from routine spaying and neutering to advanced soft tissue, dental, and orthopedic operations, all monitored with modern anesthetic equipment.

Boarding

Going away? Leave your furry friend in trusted hands. Our comfortable, hygienic, and supervised boarding facilities ensure your pet feels right at home with daily playtime and loving attention.

Treatment & Emergency

Whether it’s a sudden illness or accident, our experienced veterinary team provides prompt diagnostics, on-site laboratory testing, and compassionate tailored care 24/7.

Vaccinations

Protect your dogs and cats from common and fatal infectious diseases with our comprehensive vaccination schedules — including Rabies, DHLPP, Feline Panleukopenia, and deworming.

Pet Care Tips

Helpful advice to keep your pet healthy and happy

Nutrition Matters

Feed your pet a balanced diet appropriate for their age, size, and health condition. Consult with us for personalized recommendations.

Regular Exercise

Ensure your pet gets daily exercise to maintain a healthy weight and prevent behavioral issues. Tailor activities to their breed and age.

Preventive Care

Regular check-ups, vaccinations, and parasite prevention are crucial for your pet's long-term health.
